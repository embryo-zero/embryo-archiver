# Embryo Archiver

Embryo Archiver is Embryo-Zero's fork of [heritrix3](https://github.com/internetarchive/heritrix3) (13c6572, commit 13c6572). It does Internet Archive's web archiving crawler.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
